Scope

This document shows Kalman filter results using current and next year’s SPF forecasts.

This document compares filtered estimates of GDP growth at quarterly and monthly frequency, in two plots. The first plot shows the monthly estimates at their native frequency. The second plot aggregates them to quarterly frequency by averaging the three monthly estimates within each quarter.

Each value is plotted at the end of its period: monthly values on the last day of the month, quarterly values and actuals on the last day of the quarter; tick marks on the x-axis similarly correspond to the last day of the quarter. Points mark the individual values in addition to the connecting lines, to make them easier to read. Alternating grey and white background shading separates the quarters.
